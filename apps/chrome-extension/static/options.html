impact log

Settings

The extension only prepares drafts. Your entries are stored and encrypted in impact log.

Drafts open here. For local development: http://localhost:5173

Default impact

Preselected in the popup and used for context-menu captures.

Privacy

The extension makes no network requests and collects no statistics. Drafts reach impact log inside the link fragment (after #), which browsers never send to servers.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab — the title, address and selected text of the current tab, only after you click the extension or its menu item.
  • scripting — read the selected text on that page at that moment. No scripts stay on pages.
  • contextMenus — the “Save … to impact log” items in the right-click menu.
  • storage — these settings (synced by Chrome) and an unsent popup draft (kept in memory until the browser is closed).
